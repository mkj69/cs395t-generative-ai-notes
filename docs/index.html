04 / RESEARCH METHOD

Research notes earn
their claims.

Learning notes use manual reconstruction. Research notes go further: their assumptions, evidence, derivations, and uncertainty must be inspectable.

Open the research writing guide →
  1. 01

    Question

    Name the tension precisely enough that an answer could be wrong.

  2. 02

    Intuition

    Build a small mental model before leaning on formal notation.

  3. 03

    Derivation

    Expose assumptions and intermediate steps, including the ones that fail.

  4. 04

    Evidence

    Separate what a source demonstrates from what you infer across sources.

  5. 05

    Connection

    Ask how the idea changes when viewed through another paper or framework.

  6. 06

    Opening

    End with a testable question, not a ceremonial conclusion.

05 / ABOUT

A learning and research memory,
kept in public.

This is

  • an evolving record of manual learning and original reasoning;
  • a place for careful derivations and useful diagrams;
  • a bridge from coursework to research questions;
  • a notebook allowed to preserve uncertainty.

This is not

  • a mirror of lecture slides or assigned PDFs;
  • a comprehensive guide to the course;
  • a collection of polished claims without evidence;
  • an implication that unwritten notes already exist.

The notebook starts with honest reconstruction.

“Write what you can reconstruct. Mark what you cannot.”
